About K B Industries

Company Overview

Incepted in the year 2007, We K B Industries, are engaged in manufacturing a wide range of Corrugated, Printed, Unprinted, and Laminated Varnish Boxes which are extensively used in packaging, industrial and agricultural sectors.

We possess a modern manufacturing facility in 6000 sq/feet area, which includes sophisticated machines and advanced technologies. Our motto is providing premium quality range of packaging products in diverse specifications.

We are progressing with tremendous speed to maintain pace with the international trend of the market.
